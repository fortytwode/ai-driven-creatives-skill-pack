# Drafting Checklist

Run this BEFORE any AI generation. Drafting is where the strategy lives. If a brief leaves this checklist with any item incomplete, the AI will fill the gap with its training-data defaults, and the output will be sloppy.

## The checklist

- [ ] **Angle is locked.** The concept maps to one of the 3-5 messaging pillars for the brand. Without this lock, the AI generates angle-less variants. (For a fitness app, that might be BodyTransformation, MentalDiscipline, ConvenienceWin, SocialAccountability, IdentityShift.)

- [ ] **Hook layers are specified separately.** Text hook, visual hook, spoken hook. Each does different work. Conflating them is the most common drafting failure.

- [ ] **Text hook is written to spec.**
  - First-person POV
  - Conversational, not staccato
  - Curiosity gap (setup, not reveal)
  - Specific numbers and stakes where possible
  - 14 to 20 words
  - No product name in the hook

- [ ] **Visual hook is specified as a theme, not a single image.** Themes survive iteration; single images run out after one test. ("Premium object in a state of waste" is a theme. "Card on fire" is one image.)

- [ ] **Format is fixed.** Discovery, Transformation, Progression, Split-screen, Listicle, or Wall of Text. Format is set at brief time, not generation time.

- [ ] **Body copy structure is BANP.** Before / After / Now / Punchline. 45 to 65 words. Conversational, first-person. NOT PAS, BAB, or AIDA.

- [ ] **Negative space is listed.** What the AI is NOT allowed to do. No faces. No actor looking shocked. No invented stats. No stock-music-sounding tracks. No off-brand props or palette. The forbidden list is the brief.

- [ ] **Audience is specific.** Not "everyone who'd want this app." A named audience: "ESL professionals", "parents of neurodivergent kids", "marathon runners with insomnia", "freelancers managing five clients."

## Failure modes to catch

- Brief that says "make it feel premium" without naming what premium looks like
- Brief that names a competitor and says "like them but better"
- Brief that asks for hooks AND scripts AND visuals in one prompt
- Brief without negative space (the forbidden list)
- Brief that defaults to "cinematic" or "photorealistic" without specifying style

If the brief fails any item, fix it before generating. The quality ceiling of any AI output is set the moment you write the brief.
