# Curation Checklist

Run this AFTER generation, BEFORE shipping. Curation is where taste lives. Most AI output is graveyard; the team's job at this stage is selection, not validation. Cut to ten percent.

## The checklist

- [ ] **Each variant read aloud.** If it sounds like ad copy, kill it. If it sounds like a person talking, keep it. AI-generated copy fails this test more often than it passes.

- [ ] **Hook layers checked for redundancy.** If the text hook and the spoken hook reveal the same fact, kill one. The setup-and-reveal split has to hold.

- [ ] **No fabricated specifics.** AI invents stat percentages, customer counts, and dollar amounts when it has nothing else to say. Every number in the variant has to be defensible.

- [ ] **Faces (if any) checked.** AI faces still fail eye contact in DR placements. The uncanny valley costs you the first second. If the variant uses a face, look at the eyes. If they don't track or feel off, kill the variant. Most performant AI-driven work is faceless for this reason.

- [ ] **Safe zone checked.** Headline overlay does not overlap the like button, the caption area, or the share button. If it does, the platform crops it on screen and the ad looks broken.

- [ ] **Brand consistency checked.** Color palette, typography, logo placement, voice tone all match the brand's existing creative system. AI drifts toward generic; the curator pulls it back.

- [ ] **Cut to ten percent.** Most AI output is graveyard. Ship the best one to two variants per format per concept. Kill the rest before they touch an ad account.

## The "would I run this against my best historical creative?" test

For every variant that survives the checks above, ask: would I run this directly against my current top-performer? If the answer is no, kill it. The AI variant that "looks fine" is the variant that dilutes your learning. Only ship variants you'd put in the same auction as your winners.

## Failure modes to catch

- Shipping everything that rendered (because generation was so cheap it felt wasteful to throw outputs away)
- Confusing aesthetic quality with performance quality (beautiful AI that quietly dies)
- Skipping the read-aloud test because the script "looks fine on the page"
- Letting AI fabrications through (specific dollar amounts, customer counts, percentages with no source)
- Approving variants with AI faces that fail eye contact
- Not verifying the safe zone in the platform's native preview tool

The reason humans matter at this stage: the AI does not know the brand, does not know what's failed in past tests, and does not know which DR principles are non-negotiable. It produces variants. You decide which variants represent the brand and which ones don't.

## After curation

Label each shipping variant with concept ID, batch ID, and variant ID so the test results are attributable. Without consistent naming, the data is unreadable later.
