# Gemini 3 Pro Image: Cluttered Desk Pain-Point Shot

A photorealistic prompt for Gemini 3 Pro Image. Renders a cluttered, anxiety-inducing scene that the viewer instantly recognizes as their own life. Pairs with productivity, sleep, mental wellness, and finance verticals.

## How to use

Paste into Gemini 3 Pro Image. Substitute the specific objects to fit your vertical's pain points. The more specific the cluttered objects, the more emotionally resonant the scene.

## The prompt

```
Top-down photograph of a chaotic home office desk at 11pm, lit by a single warm desk lamp from the upper left and the cool blue glow of a smartphone screen showing 47 unread notifications. On the desk: a half-drunk cup of coffee with a ring stain on a paper napkin, three crumpled sticky notes with handwriting, an open laptop with too many browser tabs, a stack of unopened bills, a tangled phone charger, a closed paper planner. Realistic textures, slight motion blur on the steam from the coffee, photo-real lighting, 1080x1920 vertical aspect ratio. Do NOT include: any human, any hands, any face, any logos, any readable brand names, any clean minimalist desk aesthetic, any plants, any stock-photo styling, any soft pastel palette.
```

## Variants by vertical

- **Sleep app:** swap the desk for a nightstand at 3am, with a glass of water, a book face-down, a phone showing the time, a bottle of melatonin.
- **Finance app:** swap to a kitchen table with bills, receipts, a calculator, a cup of coffee, a laptop showing a banking dashboard.
- **Fitness app:** swap to a bedroom floor with workout clothes piled up, an unmade bed, a half-eaten protein bar, a phone showing a missed alarm.
- **Mental wellness app:** swap to a bathroom counter at 7am with a phone face-up showing notifications, an open prescription bottle, a coffee cup, a crumpled to-do list.
