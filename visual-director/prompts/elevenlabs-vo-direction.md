# ElevenLabs VO Direction

A 25-second voiceover prompt for ElevenLabs, with explicit emotional direction. The script uses the BANP structure and a conspiratorial tone (warm, slightly amused, like a friend telling a secret).

## How to use

In ElevenLabs, select your cloned voice. Apply the voice settings below. Paste the script into the prompt field. Generate, then trim or re-cut individual lines as needed.

## Voice settings

- Voice: cloned voice (or a stock voice that matches "warm, conspiratorial, slightly amused")
- Stability: ~40 (lower for more emotional range)
- Similarity: ~75 (higher for closer match to the source clone)
- Style exaggeration: low
- Pacing: relaxed, with a clear breath before the punchline

## Emotional direction

"Warm, conspiratorial, slightly amused, like a friend telling a secret over coffee. Low and steady, never urgent. Slight rise on the punchline."

## The script (BANP structure, sleep tracking app example)

```
You know that thing where you wake up at 3 a.m. and your brain is suddenly running a TED talk about an email from last Tuesday? Yeah. For two years I thought I was just bad at sleeping. Turns out I was bad at knowing what was wrecking my sleep. The screen time, the late coffee, the room that was three degrees too warm. Once I could see the pattern, I could actually fix it. Took about a week. The app does not put you to sleep. It just shows you why you are not sleeping. Most people figure it out in their first three nights.
```

## Adapting for your vertical

Replace the script with your own BANP-structured copy. Keep the conspiratorial tone but tune the emotional register to your category:

- **Finance app:** warm but slightly self-deprecating. ("I checked my bank balance before every coffee for two years before I figured out the problem wasn't the coffee.")
- **Fitness app:** conversational, slightly amused, never preachy. ("I trained five days a week for a year and didn't see my abs. Turns out my workout was fine. My kitchen wasn't.")
- **Meditation app:** lower energy, slower pacing, more pauses. ("I used to lie in bed at 1am doing breathing exercises I'd read on Reddit.")
- **Productivity app:** medium energy, slightly conspiratorial, drop the punchline crisply. ("My coworkers think I have a photographic memory. I don't correct them.")

Generate 3 to 5 takes per script. Trim and re-cut individual lines. ElevenLabs sometimes drifts on long takes; cutting line-by-line and recombining often produces a tighter final.
