# Sora 2 Transformation Clip

A six-second Before-to-After video for Sora 2. The transformation format is one of the proven static and short-form video conventions for subscription apps: viewer sees the bad state, then the good state, with a clean match-cut between them.

## How to use

Paste into Sora 2. Substitute the bracketed bad-state and good-state to fit your vertical. Keep the match-cut structure.

## The prompt

```
A six-second vertical 9:16 video. First three seconds: a tense woman seen only from behind, hunched at a cluttered desk in a dim apartment, shoulders tight, the warm-cool color clash of a desk lamp and a laptop screen on her face (face never visible), papers and a phone vibrating on the desk. Camera holds steady on a medium shot. At the three-second mark, a smooth match-cut to: the same woman from behind, now standing at a tall window in soft natural morning light, shoulders relaxed, holding a plain ceramic mug, looking out at out-of-focus trees. Camera pushes in slowly over the final three seconds. Pacing: hold-cut-push. Cinematic, naturalistic, color graded warm. Do NOT include: visible face, on-screen text, music suggestions, any meditation app UI, any other people, any stock-yoga imagery, any spa cliches, any candles or incense.
```

## Variants by vertical

- **Sleep app:** bad state is a person tossing in bed at 3am with phone glow on the ceiling; good state is the same person sleeping peacefully with the phone face-down on the nightstand.
- **Finance app:** bad state is a person at a kitchen table surrounded by bills, head in hands; good state is the same person calmly pouring coffee, with a single phone showing a green dashboard on the counter.
- **Fitness app:** bad state is a person sitting on the bedroom floor in workout clothes, defeated; good state is the same person mid-stride on a sunlit sidewalk.
- **Productivity app:** bad state is a person typing furiously on a laptop late at night; good state is the same person closing the laptop early and standing up.

Always keep the face hidden. AI faces still fail eye contact in DR placements.
